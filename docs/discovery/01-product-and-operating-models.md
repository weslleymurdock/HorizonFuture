# Product and operating models

## Vision

HorizonFuture should help an NGO explain, with evidence, how its mission
becomes services, projects, results, and accountability. The differentiator
is not registering people in isolation, but connecting:

`resource origin → funding instrument → project → approved budget
→ allocation → commitment → financial movement → evidence → reconciliation
→ accountability → audit`

The financial connection does not replace the social connection: delivered
services, participants, activities, indicators, and results must remain
relatable without duplicating people or inventing impact from counts.

## Product objectives

1. Provide visibility into social operations and projects in progress.
2. Preserve the origin, restrictions, and use of every resource.
3. Support budgets, commitments, and cash flow without confusing forecasts
   with payments.
4. Produce reproducible accountability reports from versioned records and
   evidence.
5. Enable intermittent operational work, with sensitive decisions
   revalidated by the server.
6. Protect personal, child and adolescent, financial, and documentary data by
   purpose and necessity.

## Operating models to validate

| Profile | Typical needs | Configuration that must not be assumed |
|---|---|---|
| Small community | few users, volunteers, simple cash, local activities | number of units, formal segregation, and document volume |
| Educational | courses, enrollments, attendance, educators, guardians, and classes | that all service delivery is school-based |
| Social assistance | eligibility, families, referrals, and benefits | that the system is a clinical record or unrestricted social assessment |
| Cultural | workshops, events, artists, audiences, and cultural deliverables | that social indicators equal educational indicators |
| Health and well-being | preventive actions, services, and referrals | collecting health data without purpose, legal basis, and professional review |
| Environmental | territorial projects, campaigns, volunteers, and environmental indicators | that an individual beneficiary is always the operational center |
| Multi-project | portfolio, teams, shared costs, and multiple funders | that a project has a single source |
| Multi-unit | locations, departments, permissions, and site-level inventory/assets | that an internal unit is a tenant |
| Predominantly public | calls, instruments, installments, and formal accountability | that all calls have the same obligations |
| Predominantly private | donations, recurring giving, campaigns, and relationships | that donor, source, promise, and receipt are the same fact |
| Hybrid | combination of the models above | that one resource rule is global for every instrument |

Likely universal capabilities: organization, people/relationships, projects,
activity records, financial sources and movements, evidence, access control,
and auditing. Forms, indicators, restrictions, approvals, calendars, and
reports should be configurable by operation or instrument, without creating a
different model for each NGO.

## Personas and objectives

| Persona | Objective | Risk to control |
|---|---|---|
| Leadership/board | decide priorities, authorize, and demonstrate governance | conflicts of interest and excessive authority |
| Project coordinator | execute plan, track goals and evidence | activity recorded as proven impact |
| Educator/social team | record service, participation, and attendance | improper exposure of family data |
| Fundraising | track opportunity, proposal, and relationship | confusing approval with received funds |
| Finance | control budget, obligations, payments, and sources | treating a commitment as a payment |
| Reconciler | match statement, movement, and adjustments | duplication or adjustment without a trail |
| Accountability preparer | assemble, review, submit, and correct package | report without a snapshot of the data used |
| Volunteer | perform authorized activity | broad access for convenience |
| Donor/funder | receive evidence and expected results | disclosure beyond the agreement or purpose |
| External accountant/advisor | review management information and obligations | product being interpreted as statutory accounting |
| Installation administrator | maintain users, units, and configuration | replacing business authorization with a technical role |

## Capability-level competitive analysis

The Bússola Social public pages referenced in the roadmap describe, at product
level, capabilities such as social-investment project management, calls,
evaluation, indicators, monitoring, budgets, accounts, categories, transfers,
attachments, and exports. This is evidence of market problems, not an internal
specification or a copy of a proprietary model.

| Public reference capability | NGO problem | HorizonFuture to consider | MVP | Differentiation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Projects, indicators, and monitoring | prove execution and results | objective–activity–evidence–indicator chain | Must | connect social service and resource |
| Calls and selection | do not miss opportunity or deadline | opportunity, application, evaluation, and decision | Must | link to instrument and budget |
| Accounts, categories, and budget | know balance by project/source | planned, committed, and realized execution | Must | restriction and traceability |
| Transfers and multiple sources | allocate costs without double counting | auditable allocations and splits | Must | origin through accountability |
| Evidence and export | answer the funder | versioned, reproducible package | Must | snapshot and reconciliation |
| Fundraising automation | scale relationships | evaluated campaigns and integrations | Later | do not expand MVP without validation |

Source: [Bússola Financeiro](https://lp.bussolasocial.com.br/bussolafinanceiro) and
[social investment management](https://lp.bussolasocial.com.br/sistema-de-gestao-para-investimento-social),
consulted as public references. Validation of MVP suitability remains internal
and is not a complete benchmark.
